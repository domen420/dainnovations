Co-Entrant Waveguides

Single Flare Waveguide Science

By placing driver ports along the horn flare where the acoustic impedance of the horn matches each driver bandwidth, low, mid, and high frequencies sum into a seamless single point-source before leaving the mouth. The outcome is 140+ dB of clean, un-distorted output with uniform polar coverage across the entire venue.
