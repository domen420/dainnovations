Multiple Entry Horn

DA46

4-Way Full-Range Horn Loudspeaker

The DA46 is D&A Innovations’ compact full-range horn for long-throw reinforcement, fills and modular arrays, where controlled coverage matters as much as output.

Four bands share one 40° x 60° horn, all driven by B&C transducers: two 12NDL88 12" low-frequency drivers, two 5NSM38 5" midrange drivers, and a DCX354 dual-diaphragm coaxial compression driver covering the high and very-high bands. Each band enters the flare at a position chosen for phase alignment at crossover, so the four sections combine into a single source with consistent response off axis.

Neodymium drivers and a fourth band give the DA46 more output and headroom than a conventional 3-way horn of the same size. Cabinets pair side by side for wider arrays.

Engineering & Waveguide Technology

Single-Source Horn

Low, mid and high bands enter one horn at positions matched to their wavelengths at crossover. The bands sum in phase and leave the mouth as one wavefront, keeping the response consistent across the coverage angle.

Controlled 40° x 60° Pattern

The narrow horizontal pattern keeps energy on the audience and off walls, holding clarity at long throw distances. Cabinets pair side by side to widen coverage without comb filtering between boxes.

Structural Integrity & Rigging

Built from 18 mm Baltic birch with internal bracing and a protective polyurea coating. Multiple M10 rigging points for flown or ground-stacked deployment.

Precision point-source speakers, multiple entry horns, and servo subwoofers engineered in Ljubljana, Slovenia.
